Our Mission

Forever forward

We believe in challenging the status quo of urban mobility. By combining cutting-edge technology with timeless design, we create electric bikes that are not just a mode of transport, but a statement of intent.

Every component is meticulously chosen, every line purposefully drawn. We design for the future of our cities, where silence, speed, and sustainability intersect seamlessly.

Our Heritage

Born in Amsterdam, the cycling capital of the world, our roots are deeply embedded in bike culture. We draw inspiration from the relentless efficiency of Dutch design and the vibrant pulse of modern European cities.

Being part of a larger mobility family allows us to innovate at scale while maintaining the uncompromising quality and attention to detail of a boutique brand.
